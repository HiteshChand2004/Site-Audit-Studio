What the best content sites do:
- Reading comes first: a body text measure of 60–75 characters, 17–20 px body size, line height around 1.6, strong headings.
- Home: featured article, latest articles, categories or topics, newsletter signup.
- Article page: title, summary, author and date, reading time, a table of contents for long pieces, related articles.
- Structured data: Article or BlogPosting with author, datePublished, headline, image; BreadcrumbList.
- Visual tone: quiet, typographic, high contrast text, minimal chrome.
