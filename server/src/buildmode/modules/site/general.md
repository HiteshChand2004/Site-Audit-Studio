General principles of an excellent site:
- Every page answers three questions within seconds: what is this, is it for me, what do I do next.
- One primary action per page, repeated at the end. Sections alternate rhythm (text, visual, proof) to keep attention.
- Real, specific copy beats generic claims; short paragraphs; headings that say something.
- Structured data: Organization and WebSite on the homepage, plus the most specific type that fits each page.
