What the best SaaS sites do:
- Hero: a specific value proposition (outcome, not feature list) in under 10 words, a one-sentence explanation, one primary
  call to action (start free / book a demo) and a secondary one, plus a product visual.
- Logo strip or a single strong proof point right below the hero.
- Problem → solution → how it works (3 steps) → key features (grid of 3–6 with icons) → proof (testimonials, metrics,
  case studies) → pricing summary → FAQ → final call to action.
- Pricing page: 2–4 plans, the recommended plan highlighted, monthly/yearly toggle when relevant, a feature comparison table,
  and an FAQ about billing. Prices from the brief only; otherwise placeholders.
- Clear navigation: Product, Pricing, Customers or Resources, Docs if any, Sign in, and the primary action as a button.
- Structured data: Organization, WebSite, SoftwareApplication (with offers when prices are known), FAQPage.
- Visual tone: crisp, confident, lots of contrast between sections, product screenshots framed consistently.
