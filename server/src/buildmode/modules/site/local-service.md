What the best local service sites do:
- First screen: what you do, where ("Family dentist in Pune"), the main action (book, call, get a quote) and a trust signal
  (years in business, rating, certification) when it was given; otherwise a promise the business can keep.
- Services: one card per service with a short outcome-focused description and a link to its own page when there are details.
- Why us: 3–4 concrete reasons; the team and reviews only when real names and reviews were given.
- Location and hours on every page footer; phone as a tel: link; a simple contact or booking form.
- Structured data: the most specific LocalBusiness subtype (Dentist, HairSalon, Plumber, LegalService, …) with address, geo,
  telephone, openingHoursSpecification, areaServed.
- Visual tone: trustworthy, clean, human photos, calm colours with one clear action colour.
