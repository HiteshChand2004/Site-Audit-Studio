What the best restaurant sites do:
- The first screen sells the experience: one large, appetising photo or video, the name, a one-line promise, and a "Book a
  table" (or "Order online") button. Opening hours and the neighbourhood are visible without scrolling on a phone.
- The menu is one tap from anywhere, readable as HTML text (never only a PDF or an image), grouped by course, with prices and
  dietary marks (V, VG, GF) explained once.
- Location block: address as text, an embedded map or a link to one, parking / transit notes, phone as a tel: link.
- Social proof only when real quotes, ratings or press were given; otherwise leave it out (never invent or placeholder
  reviews) and let the story, the food and the place carry trust.
- Booking or ordering is the primary action on every page; a sticky button on phones.
- Structured data: Restaurant (or CafeOrCoffeeShop / BarOrPub) with address, geo, telephone, openingHoursSpecification,
  servesCuisine, priceRange, menu URL, acceptsReservations.
- Visual tone: warm, photographic, generous imagery; typography with character for headings, very readable body text.
