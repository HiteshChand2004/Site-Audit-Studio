What the best portfolios do:
- Lead with the work, not the biography: a short positioning line ("Brand designer for climate startups") and the best 3–6
  projects as large visual cards above the fold or right after it.
- Each project page tells a story: context, the problem, the role, the process (a few images), the outcome with numbers.
- An about section with a real photo, a short human paragraph, skills or services, and clients.
- One obvious way to get in touch (email link or a short form) on every page.
- Structured data: Person (or Organization for a studio), with sameAs links to the profiles given in the brief.
- Visual tone: the design itself is part of the portfolio — distinctive typography, restrained palette, precise spacing.
