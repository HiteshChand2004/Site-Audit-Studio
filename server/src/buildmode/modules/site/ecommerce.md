What the best shop sites do:
- Home: hero with the hero product or collection and one shopping action, then categories, bestsellers, the brand promise
  (shipping, returns, quality), reviews, and a newsletter signup.
- Product cards: image, name, price, rating, a quick view or add action; consistent image ratios.
- Product page: large gallery, title, price, variant selectors, add to cart above the fold, delivery and returns summary,
  description, specs, reviews; related products.
- Trust everywhere: secure checkout note, delivery times, returns policy link, contact.
- Cart and checkout need a backend: build the pages and buttons, and list "payments and orders" as manual work in finish.
- Structured data: Product with offers, aggregateRating (only from real data), BreadcrumbList, Organization.
- Visual tone: product photography first, clean backgrounds, clear prices, fast and calm.
