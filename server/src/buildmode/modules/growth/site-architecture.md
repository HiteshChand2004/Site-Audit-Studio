These are the Growth OS playbook rules for sites that bring in business enquiries. Where a rule here conflicts with the safety rules, the safety rules win.

**MUST have:**
- **Home**: buyer's outcome as the H1, the areas served (max 3 tabs or groups), how it works (3 plain steps), proof, FAQ, one CTA.
- **One page per offer/solution**, each targeting its own search phrase.
- **One landing page per paid-search intent** (ad group). The ad's search phrase, the ad text and the page H1 must match ("message match").
- **Contact page** with the short form.
- **About page** with the agreed brand facts.
- **Legal pages**: privacy policy (describing every tracker), terms.

**SHOULD have:**
- **Buyer guides** (articles) answering the long questions buyers ask, with FAQ schema.
- **A free, low-commitment offer page** for paid traffic, form above the fold.
- **A "who we work with / who we don't" line** on the contact page.

**Navigation:** keep the top bar short (max 6 items). Do not add a menu just for ad landing pages; link them from the relevant solution page and the footer.
