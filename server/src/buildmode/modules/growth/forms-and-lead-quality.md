These are the Growth OS playbook rules for sites that bring in business enquiries. Where a rule here conflicts with the safety rules, the safety rules win.

**The form:**
- **MUST** be short: name, work email, company, "What do you want to solve?" (a dropdown of offers), free text. Phone optional.
- **SHOULD** add **one qualifying question** that separates good-fit buyers. Poor-fit leads cost the same ad money as good ones.
- **MUST** include a hidden honeypot field against spam.
- **MUST** send attribution with every submission.
- Under the form, show 3 short proof lines.
- The thank-you state names the next step and when it happens.

**Separate buyer forms from non-buyer forms.** Newsletter, careers/expert applications and partner enquiries MUST fire different events and MUST NOT count as leads.
