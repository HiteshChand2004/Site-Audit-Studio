title: Growth OS playbook: Core principles
---
These are the Growth OS playbook rules for sites that bring in business enquiries. Where a rule here conflicts with the safety rules, the safety rules win.

1. **Every page is built for one search and one action.** A visitor arrives with a question; the page answers it first, proves it, and offers one next step.
2. **Answer first, then prove, then ask.** Order on every landing page: the answer → the proof → the form.
3. **Use the buyer's words, not ours.** Titles and H1s copy what buyers type into Google, not internal product names.
4. **Never invent a number, client, certification or standard.** Every claim comes from the claims registry.
5. **Claim only what ships.** If a capability is not live, label it "build with you" or "partner-delivered", or leave it out.
6. **Measure from the first visit.** Tracking is part of the build, not a later task.
7. **Filter, don't just attract.** The site must discourage poor-fit buyers as well as convert good ones. Paid clicks from non-buyers are wasted money.
8. **Readable by machines.** Google and AI assistants (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini) must be able to read and quote every answer in the served HTML.
