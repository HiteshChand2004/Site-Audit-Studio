Remastly MUST ask for these, and MUST NOT make them up:

| Input | Why it matters | Example (PredCo) |
|---|---|---|
| Who buys (role, company type, size) | Copy, form qualifier, proof | Plant head / safety head at multi-site manufacturers |
| Target countries | Schema `areaServed`, regional blocks, consent mode | India, USA |
| The buyer's search phrases, per offer (from Google Keyword Planner / Search Console) | Page titles, H1s, one landing page per intent | "video smoke detection camera", "restricted zone monitoring" |
| The offers / solutions, each mapped to something that ships | One page per offer; "claim only what ships" | Fire & smoke detection on existing CCTV |
| Approved claims (numbers, certifications, clients, awards) with source | The **claims registry**; nothing else may be stated | 25+ plant deployments; SOC 2; ISO 27001 |
| One primary action (the conversion) | One CTA everywhere | "Book a 30-minute working session" |
| Who is NOT a fit | Form qualifier, lead scoring, copy that filters | Single-site SMEs below a minimum budget |
| Brand facts: legal name, founding date, address, team size, social links | Must be identical everywhere (site, schema, directories) | One agreed set of numbers |
| Tracking IDs (GTM container, GA4 property) | Measurement from first visit | GTM-XXXXXXX |

**Evidence:** On indikaai.com, the website, IndiaAI and YourStory gave different team sizes and founding years, and Google suggested "Indika AI fake or real" as a related search. Inconsistent facts cost trust before the buyer reads a word.
