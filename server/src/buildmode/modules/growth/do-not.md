title: Growth OS playbook: Do not
---
- Do not invent numbers, logos, clients, reviews or certifications.
- Do not count newsletter, partner or job forms as leads.
- Do not put more than one primary action on a page.
- Do not use long forms (predco.ai had a 9-field form and 0 enquiries from ~40 real visits a month before the 4-field form).
- Do not hide answers behind JavaScript-only rendering.
- Do not redirect inside page code.
- Do not launch paid ads to a page that does not answer the exact search.
- Do not load trackers on staging or test domains.
