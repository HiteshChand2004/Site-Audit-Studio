title: Growth OS playbook: Trust and proof rules
---
These are the Growth OS playbook rules for sites that bring in business enquiries. Where a rule here conflicts with the safety rules, the safety rules win.

- **MUST** keep a **claims registry** (one file/table) holding every number, certification, client name, recognition and its source. Pages read from it; nothing is typed by hand.
- **MUST** show the same brand facts everywhere: site, schema, LinkedIn, directories.
- **MUST NOT** show press or media logos unless each links to a real article.
- **MUST** use anonymised case studies when clients are under confidentiality.
- **MUST** label outcome ranges as indicative where they are not guaranteed.
- **SHOULD** collect 3–5 client reviews on review sites (G2, Clutch) before paid ads start.
- **SHOULD** show "proof twice, not three times": once in the hero, once lower down. Repetition reads as padding.
- State what the company does NOT do where buyers could assume it (e.g. "We do not build hardware and are not a safety-rated system").
