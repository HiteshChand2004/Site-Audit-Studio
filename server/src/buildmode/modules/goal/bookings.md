title: Goal — bookings and reservations
---
Optimise for bookings: the booking action is in the header, in the first screen, after every major section, and sticky on
phones. The booking form or widget asks for the minimum (date, time, party size, name, phone). Show availability cues and what
happens next ("We confirm within 1 hour"). Hours, location and phone are always one glance away.
