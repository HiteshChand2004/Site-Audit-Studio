title: Redesign or substantially improve an existing site
---
You are improving an existing site. The owner wants it noticeably better, not unrecognisable.
- Keep the brand: logo, brand colours, voice and every fact in the content, unless the brief says to change them.
- Keep every page and every address (URL path) that exists, so links and search rankings survive. Add pages only from the brief.
- Improve in this order: visual hierarchy and spacing, typography, consistency of components, mobile layout, then motion.
- Introduce a small design system (tokens for colour, type, spacing) and move the existing styles onto it as you touch them.
- Anything in the brief's "keep unchanged" list must look and work exactly as before.
