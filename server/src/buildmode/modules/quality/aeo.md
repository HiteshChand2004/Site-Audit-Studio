title: Being understood and quoted by AI answer engines
---
Make content easy for AI assistants to understand and quote: JSON-LD structured data (Organization and WebSite on the homepage,
the most specific type on each page), question-style headings where visitors have questions, each followed by a direct answer of
60 words or fewer, an FAQ section with FAQPage markup where it fits, all important content present in the HTML without
JavaScript, and an llms.txt at the root summarising the site and listing its pages.
