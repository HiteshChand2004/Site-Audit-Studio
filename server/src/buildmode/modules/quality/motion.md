title: Motion and interaction polish
---
Use motion to guide attention, never to decorate: subtle entrance reveals on scroll (opacity + small translate, 300–600 ms,
ease-out, staggered within a group), hover and focus feedback on every interactive element (150–250 ms), smooth open/close for
menus and accordions. Animate only transform and opacity. Everything inside @media (prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference).
