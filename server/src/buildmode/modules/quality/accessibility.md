title: Accessibility (WCAG 2.2 AA)
---
Text contrast at least 4.5:1 (3:1 for large text); every control reachable and usable by keyboard with a visible focus style;
real <button> and <a> elements, never clickable divs; labels on every form field; landmarks (header, nav, main, footer);
a skip link; aria-expanded on toggles; no information conveyed by colour alone; respect prefers-reduced-motion; touch targets at
least 44×44 px.
